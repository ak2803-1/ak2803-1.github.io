01 / ABOUT

About me.

My interest in investing actually started with my love for academic trivia. In high school, I spent so much time learning why countries, industries, and the most successful people developed the way they did, which naturally led me toward economics.

In college, I became particularly interested in technology because it felt like I was watching those kinds of structural changes happen in real time. That led me to venture capital, where I got my first experience actually evaluating technology businesses.

I loved the process of learning an unfamiliar industry and forming a view on a company, but I wanted a more quantitative way to test those views. That's ultimately what drew me toward finance and investing in general.

2025

Built PrepNova

CO-FOUNDER / STUDENT PRODUCTIVITY

I hate setting up my Google Calendar. I especially hate setting it up before school starts, when I have to add every class, read through every syllabus, and manually enter all the important dates for the semester. My friends and I realized there was an easier way to do this.

We created a website that connected to an AI model, went into Canvas, parsed your syllabi, and automatically added important dates to your Google Calendar. But as we built it, we realized there were even more administrative tasks we could eliminate. We added an adaptive study scheduler that looked at what was being taught in class, created study blocks before exams, and scheduled them around events already on your calendar. We also experimented with a shared class database where students could contribute and access notes.

We solved the problem we originally set out to solve: wasting time on administrative school tasks. But building PrepNova taught me a different way to approach problems entirely: determine whether the problem needs to exist at all.

2026

Bold North Capital

PRIVATE EQUITY INTERN

As an intern for a PE firm, I had the unenviable task of finding 10–15 acquisition targets a week. My boss told me to spend my mornings scouring the internet for them. I needed to automate this. My sleep depended on it.

I paired a free web scraper in ChatGPT's Atlas with Agent Mode to scrape multiple industry sites at a time, then gave the resulting list back to ChatGPT along with our acquisition criteria memo. In 10 minutes, I had a ranked list of credible acquisition targets, saving me hours of work—and, more importantly, precious sleep.
